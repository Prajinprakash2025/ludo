    A QUICK REFRESHER

    Small board.
    Big possibilities.

    1. Six gets you started. Roll a 6 to move a token out of its nest. Entering uses the roll.
    2. Follow your color home. Tokens move clockwise. You need the exact roll to finish.
    3. Catch up, send back. Land on an opponent’s token to send it to its nest. Star squares are safe.
    4. Keep a good thing going. A six, capture, or finishing a token gives you another roll. Three sixes in a row lose the third roll and end your turn.
    5. Bring the whole crew home. First to finish all four tokens wins. Own tokens may stack; this version has no blockades.
    45 seconds per action. A missed action passes the turn. Invalid moves are blocked; unlucky rolls and bad strategy never get you kicked out.
